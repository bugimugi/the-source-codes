            Manifesto

            How this library earns trust

            1. Every claim carries its sources. No statement stands alone.
            2. Evidence is graded, never implied. Verified, supported, preliminary, historical, traditional, disputed, unverified.
            3. Tradition is documented as tradition. Cultural and historical use is shown with its origin – it is not presented as proof of effect.
            4. Missing sources are shown openly as Source pending verification.
            5. Counting is not meaning. Numerical patterns in texts are shown with their counting rules, so anyone can check or dispute them.
            6. Information, not medical advice. Nothing here replaces a doctor. Never stop a prescribed treatment without medical advice.
